Poetry by Ryan McLean

The Day Begins Anew As The Sun Sets On Yesterday

As I left I chased the sun west as though I would not let the day set

With flights weekly to commute hundreds of miles from work to coast I searched for myself

Finding nothing but regret and loss I focused on what the past held.

Upon my return I drove a calm surrender into the new sun rise

Only to return back where I started, angry and feeling of failure

All that effort to find myself undone

Yet I can’t help but see the sun set on who I was and rise on who I am to become
